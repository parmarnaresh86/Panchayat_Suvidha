# Product

PanchayatSuvidha is a digital village portal for Sayla, Gujarat. It serves as a public-facing information hub for village residents and a management tool for panchayat administrators.

## Core Purpose
- Display village profile, census data, history, and panchayat member details
- Provide a services directory covering education, employment, facilities, and government services
- Allow admins to manage content (modules, staff, announcements, forms, etc.)
- Support bilingual content in English and Gujarati

## User Roles
- **Public users**: Browse village info, services, contact details
- **Admin users**: Log in to manage all module data via the admin dashboard

## Key Modules
- Village Profile & Panchayat Details
- Services (Education, Employment, Facilities, Government)
  - Education: Primary School, Anganwadi, Library
  - Employment: Animal Husbandry, Employment Board, Market Yard
  - Facilities: PGVCL Electric, ST Bus Timetable, Water Supply, Health Center
- Form Download Center
- Staff Attendance
- Contact Page
